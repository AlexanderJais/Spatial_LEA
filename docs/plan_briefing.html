Findings · 12 sections · anatomically registered

Galanin receptor signalling in the ageing mediobasal hypothalamus

MBH Galr1 lives in a GABAergic DMH population — 96.6% of it. But its apparent age difference is inseparable from a rostro-caudal gradient so steep that one animal's own three sections span more Galr1 than the two age groups differ by.

4 male mice · 3 sections each 50,023 MBH cells aged 15–16 mo vs adult 7 mo 17 Aug 2026
Headline

The Galr1 lead does not survive the extra sections

Per-animal means look convincing: aged 51.3% and 70.5% of cells Galr1⁺, adult 73.5% and 80.9%, a 16.3-point gap consistent in both blocks. The per-section values are not. G_073 alone spans 59.9–80.4% — a 20.5-point swing inside one animal — and its most rostral section sits above three of the six adult sections.

Galr1 detection correlates r = +0.85 (p < 0.001) with a cell-composition axis, and that correlation holds within animals, where age is constant and any slope must be anatomical. The same axis separates the groups perfectly, so at n = 2 per group age and section plane are fully collinear and cannot be told apart.

A negative result for the receptor — and a specific, fixable finding about study design.

The anatomy question, answered

Galr1 is concentrated in a GABAergic DMH population

The cluster carrying Galr1 as a defining marker — GABAergic, co-expressing Gal, Foxp2 and Th, 2,338 cells — is 96.6% DMH (2,259 DMH, 61 VMH, 18 ARC). So MBH galanin-receptor signalling is a DMH story, in a galanin-co-expressing inhibitory population, and 46–83% of those cells carry Galr1 transcript against 0.07–0.09% for negative-control probes.

That population is the right target for orthogonal validation: real, well-defined, Galr1-rich, and anatomically where the study expected to look.

Method

The drawn rectangles are gone

Every section now carries a coordinate frame built from its own anatomy — ml from the third-ventricle midline, dv from the ventral surface. The ventricle is traced from its own lining (Gpr50 tanycytes, Spag16 ependyma), seeded at the arcuate so it cannot grab a lateral ventricle; the arcuate also fixes which way is ventral, so mounting angle stops mattering. ARC, VMH and DMH are fitted once in that shared frame and applied to all 12 sections.

What survives is in the ligand, not the receptor

Next, in order of value

What would make this publishable

  1. Match sections on rostro-caudal position

    The single highest-value change. The composition axis built here already works as an AP proxy; with the DAPI images now in hand, Allen CCF registration would give a calibrated one. Without matching, a 20-point Galr1 swing is available for free.

  2. Re-test the Gal effects against that axis

    Exactly the test that dismantled the Galr1 lead. If they survive it, the ligand story is real and worth building on.

  3. More animals

    At n = 2 per group, any axis that happens to separate the four animals is perfectly collinear with age — as happened here. n ≈ 5–8 per group breaks that collinearity and makes the contrast estimable.

  4. Validate the DMH Gal/Galr1 population by RNAscope

    In AP-matched sections. It is a real, Galr1-rich, well-defined DMH population regardless of the age result, and counting it directly is the cleanest orthogonal check.
