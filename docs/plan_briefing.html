One framing caveat worth pre-empting

By the NIA/JAX convention for C57BL/6 — adult 3–6 mo, middle-aged 10–14 mo, old 18–24 mo — the two "aged" animals at 15.0 and 16.1 months are middle-aged. The contrast is real and hypothalamic ageing phenotypes do begin in middle age, but the ages should appear in the abstract and the claim be scoped to mid-life onset. An 18–24 mo group later would turn the caveat into a trajectory.

Pipeline · stages run in dependency order

How the analysis proceeds

  1. QC with negative-control calibration

    The 27 negative-control probes and 41 negative-control codewords give a per-section false-detection rate. This sets the threshold for what counts as a real Galr1 transcript — the question always asked of a low-abundance GPCR.

  2. Does the tissue actually look older?

    Gfap, Cd68, Trem2, Spp1, Cd44 should rise in the aged animals. Run early and cheaply — if the canonical ageing signature is absent, that reframes everything downstream.

  3. Cell typing by three independent routes

    Label transfer from HypoMap (384,925 hypothalamic cells), from the Allen ABC atlas, and unsupervised Leiden with manual marker annotation. Cells kept where routes agree; concordance reported.

  4. Nucleus assignment, also triangulated

    The panel lacks Nr5a1, Tbx3 and Trh, so markers alone cannot call nuclei. Allen CCF registration, ABC-atlas spatial mapping and Banksy spatial domains, reconciled — plus a continuous frame (distance to 3rd ventricle, dorsoventral depth) that is immune to registration error.

  5. The Galr1 analysis

    Three separable claims, tested separately — see below. Run in parallel for Galr3 and the ligand Gal, because a shift in ligand–receptor balance is a stronger result than a receptor count.

  6. Spatial and circuit analyses

    Gal→Galr1 neighbourhood coupling, Galr1 as a spatial gradient along the ventricular axis, niche composition around Galr1⁺ DMH neurons. This is what separates a spatial paper from a dissociated-tissue paper.

  7. Robustness battery

    Leave-one-animal-out, section-level replication, exact blocked permutation, threshold and segmentation sensitivity, negative-control genes, and a power curve for n = 2/3/5/8. Reported whether or not it is flattering.

The intellectual core

"Galr1 changed" is three different claims

Reporting a shift without separating these is the most common way an in-situ receptor paper gets sent back.

A
A different fraction of cells expresses Galr1.Per-animal detection rate per cell type × nucleus, against a background-calibrated threshold.
B
The same cells express more or less Galr1.Pseudobulk per animal × cell type × nucleus; negative binomial with a total-count offset, ~ age + batch.
C
The abundance of Galr1-high cell types changed.Compositional analysis — a different finding entirely, and one that can masquerade as A or B.
Statistics

The honest power position

With 2 vs 2 in a blocked design, the exact permutation null has only four label assignments. The observed assignment and its mirror are indistinguishable, so the smallest attainable exact two-sided p-value is 0.5 — for any gene, however large the effect. No analysis choice changes this.

What this cohort delivers: a rigorously annotated spatial atlas, per-animal effect sizes with confidence intervals, replication across two independent batches and across sections, and a ranked shortlist of cell type × nucleus hypotheses.

What a high-impact claim additionally needs: the second cohort as independent replication, or more animals (n ≥ 5 per group makes standard pseudobulk testing meaningful), or orthogonal validation of top hits by RNAscope — which such journals expect for an in-situ receptor claim regardless of n.
